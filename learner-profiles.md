title: Learner Profiles
---

This course is for complete beginners, mostly working adults in office jobs who want to change career or move into programming or computing. These profiles are examples, not real people.

## Administration officer moving into IT

Olivia, 34, is an administration officer in Perth. She is confident with email, Word and Excel but has never written code. She wants to move into an IT support or junior developer role. She studies in the evenings and at weekends, so the 6-hour weekly cap and short videos matter to her. She also wants credit she can count towards a qualification later.

## Finance clerk who wants to automate reports

Noah, 45, works in accounts and spends hours each month copying figures between spreadsheets. He wants to write small Python scripts that read files and tidy data, and eventually move into a data analyst role. Weeks 5 and 6 (files, modules and packages) and the optional pandas section are where the course pays off for him.

## Customer service team leader changing career

Emma, 29, leads a customer service team and has decided to retrain in computing. She is nervous about maths and technical words. The course assumes no background: every term is explained the first time it appears, the maths stays at everyday arithmetic, and the Concepts Bridges explain how computers and programs work in plain English.
