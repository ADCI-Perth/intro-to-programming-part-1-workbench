**Intro to Programming, Part 1** is the first of two six-week, beginner Python micro-credentials from the Australian Data and Cyber Institute (ADCI). Part 2 is a separate course. It is for **complete beginners**, especially people in office jobs who want to change career or move into programming or computing. You do not need any programming or technical background. Each week takes **6 hours or less**, so it fits around a full-time job, and the course is designed to support credit towards an introductory programming unit.

You learn mostly from short YouTube videos (the Telusko *Python for Beginners* playlist, plus a few videos from other channels), these lesson notes, and a practice task each week. You write and run all the code in **PyCharm**.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the six weeks you will be able to:

- **LO 1** Analyse and solve a problem, and implement the solution in a programming language.
- **LO 2** Apply object-oriented, imperative, functional and logic programming techniques.
- **LO 3** Design and build a small program to meet a given specification.
- **LO 4** Evaluate which programming paradigm best suits a particular problem.

## How each week works

Each week is one episode in the menu. Open a week to see its parts: the week overview, the sections (such as 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3), the Concepts Bridge and the practice task. Every week follows the same blocks:

| Block | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Watch | about 0.3 to 1.25 h | Short videos listed on the week overview |
| Read & practise | about 1 to 2 h | The week's sections: read, run every example, do the exercises |
| Concepts Bridge | 30 min | General programming ideas the Python lessons do not cover |
| Build | 2 h (3 h in Week 6) | Your portfolio item: a practice task with starter code, a model solution and tests |
| Quiz | 15 min | A short quiz, marked automatically |

Your portfolio for each week is submitted at the end of the week and marked by your tutor.
