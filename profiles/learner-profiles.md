These profiles describe the people this course is designed for. They are examples, not real people.

## Career changer

Olivia, 34, works in retail management in Perth and wants to move into a data role. She is confident with spreadsheets but has never written code. She studies in the evenings and on weekends, so the 6-hour weekly cap and short videos matter to her. She wants credit she can count towards a qualification later.

## First-year student getting ahead

Liam, 18, starts an IT degree next semester and wants to arrive already knowing the basics. He learns quickly from video but tends to skip planning. The Concepts Bridges and the pseudocode step in Week 3 are aimed at learners like him.

## Working professional automating tasks

Noah, 45, is a lab technician who spends hours copying results between files. He wants to write small scripts that read and tidy data. Weeks 5 and 6 (files, modules and packages) and the optional pandas section are where the course pays off for him.
